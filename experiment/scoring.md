# scoring transfer responses

primary outcome = mean transfer score. do not score recall into the primary outcome.

## scale

score each transfer answer 0–2 independently.

- **2 — correct mental model:** predicts the changed outcome and gives the causal reason the concept requires. wording does not need to match the stimulus.
- **1 — partial:** gets the main direction right but misses a necessary consequence, confuses a secondary detail, or gives an incomplete causal explanation.
- **0 — incorrect:** predicts the wrong outcome, gives a contradictory explanation, or shows no usable mental model.

## scoring rules

- score what the participant explains, not how much terminology they use
- do not reward recall of exact phrases from the stimulus
- do not penalise grammar, spelling, or concise answers when the causal model is correct
- if an answer contains both a correct and incorrect explanation, score the overall mental model conservatively at 1 unless the correct explanation clearly resolves the contradiction
- score blind to arm and participant id whenever practical

## primary analysis

for each participant, calculate the mean of their four transfer answers across the two concepts. compare arm-level means, with concept-level results shown separately.

report the raw scores and n per arm. with n=20 this is a directional probe, not a significance claim.

## recall

recall is diagnostic only. do not use it to decide whether construction improved learning.

## mixed result

apply the pre-registered rule in `experiment/README.md` exactly. do not add concepts beyond the single permitted extension.
